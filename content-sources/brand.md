# Ordergroove Brand Context — canonical

Apply everything below to all Ordergroove copy and visual work without being asked. This file is the single source of truth; if a session contradicts it, flag the conflict instead of silently picking one.

## Colors & type

- Midnight `#17132F` (primary dark bg) · Groovy Green `#00FF85` (dark bg only; use `#009A50` on light backgrounds) · Intense Cyan `#00FAFF` · Laser Lemon `#EAFF00` · Flamingo Pink `#FF61D3` · Dark Indigo `#2E2A47`
- Brand gradient: `linear-gradient(135deg, #E0FE00, #0BFE5F, #00FEFE)`
- Font: **Figtree** everywhere
- Cross-check hexes against this file, not from memory — near-miss variants (`#0b0820`, `#0B0E22`, `#14102a`) are drift, not alternatives.
- Logo-only exception (Cameron approved, 2026-10-26): preserve the supplied light-background logo with Midnight lettering and `#00CC6A` rings. Do not recolor it to `#009A50` or add `#00CC6A` to the general palette.
- On light surfaces, use Midnight for small text and links. Green `#009A50` buttons need Midnight labels; white labels do not meet normal-text contrast requirements.
- Hub CTA buttons (Cameron approved, 2026-10-26): Midnight `#17132F` fill with white text; invert to white fill with Midnight text on hover. Keep a visible border and keyboard-focus ring. Use the shared button component for hero actions, shortcuts, downloads, sign-in, and other CTAs.

## Positioning

- Ordergroove is **"the enterprise standard for subscriptions and recurring revenue."**
- Lead message is a category move — **Autonomous Subscriptions** — never a feature list. Ladder DOWN from the category shift, not UP from features.
- Category definition: *"Subscription infrastructure that runs itself. Built for every channel, trained on the largest enterprise subscription network, and able to test and grow faster than any team could alone."*
- Products: **Agents** (the autonomous layer: Payment Agent, Retention Agent, Analytics Agent) · **Workflows** (orchestration) · **Open Platform** (GraphQL API, Ordergroove MCP server).
- Rights-to-Win, in this locked order:
  1. **Everychannel Infrastructure.** *One platform. Everychannel.* Integrate once and capture every channel: storefronts, retail, voice, and agent endpoints not built yet. Scan to Subscribe is a proof point here.
  2. **The Groove Network.** *The largest enterprise subscription dataset.* Every reach, retry, recovery, and retention test is refined by the most diversified subscription data in enterprise commerce (1,000+ brands, $110B+ transaction signals, 75M+ subscriber profiles).
  3. **Testing Velocity.** *Test faster. Compound the wins.* Agents propose variants, the brand deploys at 1%, 5%, or 10%, and every winner compounds. Agents appear by name only here.
- Audience: enterprise and mid-market B2C brands with repeat purchase patterns, typically $50M+ GMV. Two buying groups: **business leaders** (VP/Director of eCommerce, VP/Head of Digital, retention, loyalty, CRM, and subscription leaders; they care about revenue, churn, and speed to market) and **technology leaders** (CTO, CIO, VP of Engineering, eCommerce platform and enterprise architecture leaders; they care about integration, scalability, data access, and readiness for an agentic stack).
- "Autonomous Commerce" is not yet cleared for external use — internal framing only.

## Hard language rules (pass/fail, not suggestions)

- "Subscriptions" must appear in any hero subhead. Never lead with "AI" or "AI agents." No "AI-powered."
- **No em-dashes anywhere.** "Everychannel" is one word.
- No "out of the box." No "heartland intelligence" (say "diversified data"). No "Relationship Commerce."
- Avoid "build" as the verb for adopting Ordergroove.
- Never name competitors (Recharge, Loop, Skio, Smartrr, Stay Ai).
- Ordergroove is 16 years old — never frame the company as "new"; only the category/release is new.
- No AI-tells: no "delve," "seamless," "innovative," "game-changing," "experience" as filler.

H1 ≤60 chars · subhead ≤140 · CTA ≤24 · wire summary 155 **characters** (not words) · type hierarchy: eyebrow 12px/0.16em tracking → H1 40–44px → subhead 21px → body 15px.
