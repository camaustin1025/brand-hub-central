# Brand Hub: Build, Don't Buy

**The decision: we're building our own brand hub.** Pilot goes live this week, before the Frontify pricing review with Lauren and Heidi.

**Why it matters:** Three teams pull brand from three sources. The drift is now visible (product screenshots and LinkedIn posts in different fonts), and our old hub is dead. One source of truth, kept current, readable by humans and AI, fixes it.

---

## By the numbers

| | Frontify | Build |
|---|---|---|
| Year 1 | $15-35K + $2-15K onboarding | ~$0 |
| 3-year total | $50-115K (3-7% annual increases) | ~$0-3K |
| Median actual contract | $32K/yr (procurement data) | n/a |
| Monthly active brand users | ~10-15 people = $1-3K per user/yr | same people, $0 |
| Setup | 3-8 weeks + we still write all content | ~1 week, ~3 hrs of my hands-on time |
| Upkeep | Admin work stays ours either way | 1-2 hrs/month |

**The big thing:** the expensive parts already exist.

- Style guide: ~70% written, just never had a home
- AI brand canon + on-brand deck/doc/LinkedIn skills: live today (Frontify's version is a gated beta)
- Build stack: same one behind our landing pages this year
- Asset library: 50-150 curated files, a size we can manage without enterprise software

---

## What we're building

| Phase | What ships |
|---|---|
| **V1 (this week)** | Guidelines site behind Google sign-in · click-to-copy colors · logo/font/token download kits · Drive + Canva links · page analytics |
| **V1 stretch** | Brand assistant chat on the homepage, answers only from our guidelines |
| **V1.5 (+1-2 wks)** | Searchable asset gallery (Claude auto-tags in our vocabulary) · connect the hub to Claude/ChatGPT so AI checks brand rules mid-task |
| **Not building** | Frontify's drag-and-drop templates module (the +$15K piece). Canva/Figma links carry it; ~$700/yr Canva Business if demand proves out |

---

## Pros vs cons

| Pros | Cons |
|---|---|
| ~$0 vs $50-115K over 3 years | Upkeep is on us (mostly me), 1-2 hrs/mo or it goes stale |
| Updates happen in the tools we already work in daily | No vendor support; we fix what breaks |
| AI layer live now, not on a vendor roadmap | Two bonus features are bleeding-edge (weeks old) |
| Homepage assistant + our vocabulary in search | No self-serve templates at launch |
| Working alternative = leverage if we ever buy | Single-maintainer risk (Liv gets backup access day one) |

**Reality check:** our old Frontify hub went stale for 3 years *inside* the paid product's free tier. The tool never fixes staleness; the workflow does. Ours updates the way we already work.

## When we'd revisit buying

Any two of these reopen it:

- 50+ people using brand assets monthly
- Agencies need weekly self-serve distribution
- Template demand outgrows the Canva path
- Hub sits stale two straight quarters
