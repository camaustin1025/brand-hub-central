# Phase 0 - Old Hub Content Audit

**Audited:** `content-sources/Brand Guidelines - Ordergroove Brand Hub.pdf` (Frontify export of the 2022-2023 hub, 40 pages, exported Sep 2025)
**Against:** `brand.md` (canon, wins every conflict) · `brand-style-guide.md` v0.1 (July 8, 2026) · `Brand vs Product Audit.md` (known drift)
**Date:** July 22, 2026 · Prepared as the Phase 0 gate. Phase 1 converts only what the carry-over manifest at the end of this doc clears.

## Bottom line

37 sections audited: **9 KEEP · 19 UPDATE · 7 RETIRE · 2 CAMERON'S CALL.**

The old hub's bones are good. The logo system, circle treatments, visual containers, and photography rules survive nearly untouched, and the neutral color ramp matches current canon value for value. Its words are mostly dead: the messaging chapter is pre-refresh top to bottom, both typography chapters describe fonts we no longer use, and every download link points into the dead Frontify site.

Three findings matter most:

1. **The green-on-white drift is real, and it is in the logo itself.** The old hub prescribes Green-400 `#00CC6A` on white (including the logo rings), its ramp contains `#009950` where canon says `#009A50`, and a pixel check of `assets/logo-white.png` confirms the shipping logo uses `#00CC6A` rings today. Three different greens on white are in circulation. Decision D1/D2 below.
2. **Key Messaging is retired wholesale.** The vision statement is built on "Relationship Commerce" (banned, rule 3.1.8) and the market-map graphic names Recharge, Skio, Stay Ai, Bold, Upscribe, Repeat, and SmartShopping.ai (violates rule 3.1.5, never name competitors). The replacement already exists: style guide sections 1.1-1.5 and 4.1.
3. **The entire asset layer needs re-homing.** Roughly a dozen asset families sit behind dead Frontify links, and every merchant logo in the example art is blocked on the legal-approved merchant list, which is still a `[TO GATHER]` in the style guide.

## How to read this

- **KEEP**: accurate as-is, cleared for Phase 1 conversion.
- **UPDATE**: structure worth keeping, content stale. The entry states exactly what changed and the current replacement.
- **RETIRE**: do not carry over. The entry cites the canon rule or fact that kills it.
- **CAMERON'S CALL**: canon is silent and it is not obviously safe. Queued in the decision list, not guessed.

Section order follows the PDF. Quoted lines from the old hub necessarily contain retired terms; quotes are audit evidence, not live copy. Rule citations (3.1.x, 5.2, etc.) point at `brand-style-guide.md`.

#### 11. Shades & Tints — UPDATE
